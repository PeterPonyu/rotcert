---
title: What is refused / not claimed
kicker: Limits
lede: Vacuous, finite-floor, and unreachable are first-class states. Several study limits are named, not hidden.
---

**DOTA exchangeability.** Overlapping crops break the scene-level i.i.d. story that conformal coverage assumes. G1 coverage on DOTA sits about 1–1.2 points below the 0.90 nominal. That is a diagnosed stress case, not a silent miss.

**G2 power-floor refusals.** Most low-support classes refuse at (β, δ) = (0.20, 0.05). HRSC is 0/1. Refusing is the protocol, not a missing table entry.

**Coverage-matched compactness is not universal.** It is a DIOR-R observation. DOTA is near parity; HRSC reverses.

**Holm-8 is descriptive.** One scalar pair per cell. It is not a license to banner “GWD always smaller.”

**Single-seed DIOR-R training.** Detector weights are not a multi-seed zoo. Extension cells use reseeds of the conformal split, not retrained detectors.

**Detector reproduction gaps.** Oriented detectors are sealed inputs. RotCert does not claim a drop-in training stack.

**Not claimed.** A new detector loss. Universal compactness of GWD. A live recomputation of conformal quantiles in the browser.
