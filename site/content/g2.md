---
title: Recall certificate
kicker: G2
lede: Learn-then-Test Hoeffding–Bentkus bounds the rotated-IoU false-negative rate, or refuses below the power floor.
---

G2 is not a second localization radius. After matching at rotated IoU ≥ 0.5, it certifies an image-level false-negative-rate bound with (β, δ) = (0.20, 0.05) as the preregistered pair.

If the realized risk sits below the LTT–HB **power floor**, G2 **refuses** rather than printing a bound it cannot back. Two refuse states appear: **finite floor** (a planning line: needs +N images, or a larger β) and **unreachable** at the chosen β.

Frozen examples at (0.20, 0.05): DOTA pilot **2/15**, DIOR-R S2A-Net **7/20**, HRSC **0/1**.
